01 · Myself

From ultrafast events to useful devices.

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Physics at IIT Hyderabad, working with Prof. Sai Santosh Kumar Raavi. My research asks how carriers, excitons, and multiexcitons evolve in nanoscale semiconductors—and how those dynamics can be deliberately engineered.

My approach combines ensemble ultrafast spectroscopy with time-resolved emission, nonlinear optical measurements, structural characterization, and single-particle photon statistics.
