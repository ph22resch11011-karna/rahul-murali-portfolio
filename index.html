01 · Myself

From ultrafast events to useful devices.

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Physics at IIT Hyderabad, working with Prof. Sai Santosh Kumar Raavi. My research asks how carriers, excitons, and multiexcitons evolve in nanoscale semiconductors—and how those dynamics can be deliberately engineered.

My approach combines ensemble ultrafast spectroscopy with time-resolved emission, nonlinear optical measurements, structural characterization, and single-particle photon statistics. The goal is a physically consistent story across timescales and length scales.

02 · Research

Current directions

01

Perovskite nanocrystals

Multiexciton & Auger dynamics

Resolving exciton, trion, and biexciton pathways with fluence-dependent femtosecond transient absorption, and designing interfaces that suppress non-radiative loss.

  • fs transient absorption
  • Global target analysis
  • Absorption cross-section
02

Quantum photonics

Single-photon emission

Connecting ensemble carrier dynamics to blinking, ON-state statistics, and photon antibunching at the single-nanocrystal level.

03

Light–matter interaction

Photonic structures

Coupling bright perovskite emitters to Bloch surface waves and studying directional emission, lifetime modification, and photon collection.

04

Charge transfer

Hot-carrier interfaces

Tracking sub-picosecond interfacial transfer and carrier cooling in perovskite–molecular heterostructures for optoelectronic devices.
